## Reading this evidence / इस जानकारी को कैसे पढ़ें

Government-reported figures are attributed claims, not independent verification. A historical audit finding does not establish present-day conditions. A missing record means verification is pending, not failure or wrongdoing. No performance score is assigned.

सरकारी प्रकाशित आँकड़ों, स्वतंत्र ऑडिट निष्कर्षों और अपनी गणना को अलग-अलग पहचानें। पुराने ऑडिट को आज की स्थिति न मानें। जानकारी उपलब्ध न होने पर “verification pending” लिखें।

## Initial evidence records

### BTE-001 — Institutional expansion / संस्थानों का विस्तार

- **Official report:** S1 states that 38 government engineering colleges and 46 polytechnic colleges have been established across all 38 districts.
- **Status:** Government-reported; institution-level operational verification pending.
- **Reference period:** Not explicitly dated in the relevant passage.
- **Interpretation:** Establishment counts do not by themselves verify adequate staff, functioning laboratories, permanent campuses or outcomes.
- **हिन्दी:** सरकार द्वारा प्रकाशित स्थापना संख्या उपलब्ध है; सभी संस्थानों की संचालन क्षमता की स्वतंत्र पुष्टि बाकी है।
- **Next evidence:** Dated departmental institution register and annual report.
- **Source:** S1.

### BTE-002 — Engineering teaching posts / इंजीनियरिंग शिक्षण पद

| Measure | Government-reported value |
|---|---:|
| Sanctioned teaching posts | 3,334 |
| Posts on which appointments are reported | 1,831 |
| Arithmetic difference | 1,503 |
| Difference as percentage of sanctioned posts | 45.1% |

- **Calculation:** (3334 - 1831) / 3334 × 100, rounded to one decimal place.
- **Status:** Attributed government figures with derived arithmetic; current vacancy count unverified.
- **Reference period:** No explicit date; source says “till now.”
- **हिन्दी:** यह स्वीकृत पदों और बताई गई नियुक्तियों का अंतर है। इसे वर्तमान रिक्ति संख्या न कहा जाए।
- **Next evidence:** Dated sanctioned, filled and working-strength statement, with definitions covering regular and contractual staff, transfers, retirements and deputations.
- **Source:** S1.

### BTE-003 — Polytechnic teaching posts / पॉलिटेक्निक शिक्षण पद

| Measure | Government-reported value |
|---|---:|
| Sanctioned teaching posts | 2,245 |
| Posts on which appointments are reported | 1,096 |
| Arithmetic difference | 1,149 |
| Difference as percentage of sanctioned posts | 51.2% |

- **Calculation:** (2245 - 1096) / 2245 × 100, rounded to one decimal place.
- **Status:** Attributed government figures with derived arithmetic; current vacancy count unverified.
- **Reference period:** 2024–25, as stated in S1.
- **हिन्दी:** 2024–25 के प्रकाशित आँकड़ों के आधार पर अंतर; अक्टूबर 2026 की working strength की पुष्टि बाकी है।
- **Next evidence:** Dated department-wide sanctioned/filled/working statement, including post categories and staff definitions.
- **Source:** S1.

### BTE-004 — Building completion / भवन निर्माण

- **Historical audit evidence:** CAG records that 18 of 46 buildings remained incomplete at the end of the scheme period, March 2021.
- **Status:** Historical audit finding; current completion status pending.
- **Scope:** Buildings under the audited ABAP expansion scheme, not all technical institutions in Bihar.
- **हिन्दी:** यह मार्च 2021 तक की ऑडिट स्थिति है। 2026 की स्थिति जानने के लिए नवीन completion और handover records चाहिए।
- **Next evidence:** Department/Building Construction Department completion and handover statements, plus the action-taken response.
- **Source:** S2, printed page 55, section 4.2.2.
